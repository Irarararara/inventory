About Me

Hi, I'm Ira!

I am a Philosophy, Politics and Economics (PPE) major at DePauw University. I am a student journalist and a podcast creator. I currently serve as Editor-in-Chief of The DePauw.

I am creative, curious and motivated. If you see an opportunity that fits, get in touch!
